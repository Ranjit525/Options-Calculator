Why Use an Options Brokerage Calculator?

Many beginner traders focus only on the gross profit (Buy Price vs. Sell Price) and forget the hidden costs. When you execute an options trade on platforms like Groww, Zerodha, or Angel One, standard flat brokerage charges (usually ₹20 per executed order) plus government taxes (STT, Stamp Duty, GST, Exchange Transaction Charges) apply.

Our tool instantly deducts a standard combined fee so you can see your Actual Net P&L. It helps you identify your true breakeven point before entering a trade.
